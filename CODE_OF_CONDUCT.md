# Code of Conduct

This project studies fairness in AI evaluation and should model it in its own community.

- Be respectful and assume good faith, especially across geographic and career-stage differences. This work is about equitable evaluation of AI globally; that standard applies here too.
- Critique methods, artifacts, and claims — not people.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination are not tolerated.
- Reports of unacceptable behavior will be handled by the maintainer (repository owner) and may result in removal of comments, blocks, or reports to GitHub.

Attribution: adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant concept, simplified for a single-maintainer research repository.
